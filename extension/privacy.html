Small footprint. Clear boundaries.

Pip is free and requires no account. The extension reads the active page only when you click to audit it. It performs the checks locally and makes no network requests. No analytics, provider keys, cookies, browsing history collection, or external AI service are used.

Your optional name, chosen illustration, work goal, and latest 20 reports are saved with chrome.storage.local on this device. Reports include the page URL, title, short evidence excerpts, findings, and capture time. They can include information visible on a signed-in page; choose carefully before exporting or sharing them. Full page HTML is never stored.

Delete saved audits or reset all local data from Field notes. Removing the extension removes its local storage. There is no cloud synchronization.

Permissions

activeTab temporarily grants access to the page when you invoke Pip. scripting reads that page's DOM or temporarily highlights matching elements. storage saves your preferences and reports. sidePanel displays the assistant. There are no all-sites host permissions.

What an audit means

The checklist score counts passed pass/fail checks. Manual review and unknown checks are excluded. It is not a ranking prediction. The extension cannot establish actual indexing, robots.txt access, response headers, backlinks, field Core Web Vitals, site-wide duplication, or rich-result eligibility. Structured data checks parse JSON and list types; they do not fully validate Schema.org requirements.

Workbench snippets are drafts. Pip never publishes changes to websites. Highlights disappear automatically and do not modify your site's saved content.

External documentation links and the website open only when you choose them. The website's URL audit sends the submitted public URL to the website server; that is separate from the local extension audit.
